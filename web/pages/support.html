Reset your password

On the login screen, select Forgot password?, enter your account email, and follow the secure link sent to you.

Delete your account

Log in, open Profile, select Delete account, and confirm. If you cannot access the app, use the account-deletion request page. Your private account data and authentication account are deleted after verification. Information required for shared trip records may remain in anonymized form.

Export trip information

Open a trip and use Export CSV from its overview.

Report a problem

Email the support address above with the app version, device model, operating-system version, steps that caused the problem, and a screenshot if available. Do not send passwords or payment credentials.
