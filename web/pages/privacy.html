TripCash

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

TripCash is a free, non-commercial app operated by Shkelqim Surkishi. This Policy explains how TripCash handles information when you use the app or website. TripCash has no advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: email address, display name, account identifier, and authentication details supplied by email, Apple, or Google. TripCash does not receive readable passwords.
  • Trips: trip and participant names, expenses, amounts, currencies, categories, notes, payments, settlements, contacts, optional PayPal.me links, and activity history. Other participants may add information about you to a shared trip.
  • Receipt scanning: when you choose Scan receipt, the selected image is sent through TripCash’s protected Cloudflare endpoint to OpenAI to create editable suggestions. TripCash does not save the image, and OpenAI requests are configured not to store it.
  • Support and security: contact-form details, authentication events, device or browser information, IP address, and security logs.
  • On your device: sessions, preferences, cached trip data, and pending offline changes.
  • Website analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics provides cookie-free, aggregated performance measurements. Google Analytics runs only if you allow optional analytics and is configured without advertising personalization or Google signals.

An email address or supported sign-in identity is required for a permanent account. Guest mode, PayPal links, saved contacts, receipt scanning, and Google Analytics are optional.

2. Why we use it

We use information to provide and secure accounts, shared trips, calculations, synchronization, invitations, requested receipt suggestions, support, abuse prevention, and service reliability. Receipt suggestions must be reviewed before saving. TripCash does not make automated financial decisions, sell personal information, use it for advertising, or track people across apps or websites.

  • Requested app features are processed to provide the service.
  • Security and essential technical information is processed to protect and maintain TripCash.
  • Optional analytics is processed only with your consent.
  • Information may be processed when required by law.

3. Sharing

Shared-trip information is visible to authorized participants. A valid invitation link shows the trip name and emoji before the person joins. Invitation links expire after 30 days.

  • Supabase provides database and authentication services.
  • Cloudflare hosts and protects TripCash, handles receipt-scan requests, and provides aggregated web analytics.
  • OpenAI processes a receipt image only when you request a scan.
  • Resend and authentication email providers deliver service and support emails.
  • Apple and Google provide optional sign-in; Google also provides consent-based website analytics.
  • An exchange-rate provider supplies rates without receiving trip records.
  • PayPal opens an optional external link; TripCash does not process payments.

We require providers to protect personal data consistently with this Policy and applicable law. Providers may process information in other countries using their available legal safeguards. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

4. Retention and deletion

Account, trip, and expense information is retained while your account remains active or until you delete eligible data. A receipt image is held only in memory long enough to complete the requested scan and is not saved to TripCash storage; the editable values you confirm become ordinary expense information. Contact messages are kept only while needed to answer and resolve the request. Security and delivery logs are retained for the limited periods applied by the relevant infrastructure provider. When you delete your account, private profile, saved contacts, memberships, and authentication account are permanently deleted and cannot be restored; shared expense records that other members still need are retained only in anonymized form. Limited records may be retained longer when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or a legal obligation, then removed when that purpose ends.

5. Your choices and rights

You can update your profile, export your data, delete eligible personal data, or permanently delete your account in Account Settings. You can also use the account-deletion request page. Change or withdraw optional analytics consent through . Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or export personal information, withdraw consent, and complain to the relevant data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Security

We use access controls, encrypted network connections, authentication, and database row-level security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We will notify affected users and relevant authorities of a personal-data breach when applicable law requires it.

7. Age

TripCash is for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly allow children under 16 to create an account. Contact us if you believe a person under 16 provided personal information.
