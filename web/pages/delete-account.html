Delete Your TripCash Account

Delete your account in the app or request deletion from TripCash Support.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Log in to TripCash.
  2. Open Profile, then Account Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm.

Request deletion without app access

Email support@tripcash.de from the email address linked to your TripCash account. Include your display name and state that you want the account deleted. Never send your password.

We verify account ownership before processing the request. Anonymous guest accounts without a linked email may only be deleted while the guest session is still accessible.

What deletion does

Your private profile, contacts, memberships, authentication account, and active sessions are deleted after the request is verified. In-app deletion normally completes during the confirmed request; support requests are completed after ownership verification. Information required to preserve shared trip and expense records may remain in anonymized form. Limited information may also be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes and removed when that purpose ends.

Sign in with Apple

If you used Sign in with Apple, TripCash deletes your account even when an Apple revocation token is not available to TripCash. After deletion, remove TripCash from Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple on your Apple device to revoke the remaining Apple authorization. This does not delay deletion of your TripCash data.
